About Link-up

Link-up is being built to help men find and connect with people nearby, while keeping control of their own data.

Using the app

Link-up is a web app: open it in your browser, or add it to your device for an app-like experience. Installation is optional.

On iPhone or iPad, open Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, look for Install app or Add to Home screen in your browser menu. On desktop, look for an install icon in the address bar or an installation option in the browser menu.

Available options depend on your browser and device. The cached introduction works offline; signing in requires a connection.
